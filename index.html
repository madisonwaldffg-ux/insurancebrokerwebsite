Common Questions

Questions I hear a lot.

What is mortgage protection?

It's a type of life insurance built around your mortgage. If you pass away, or with living benefits, become seriously ill, it helps make sure your family doesn't lose the home.

I already have life insurance through work. Isn't that enough?

Work coverage is a great thing to have, but it's usually a group policy you don't own. If you leave or change jobs, it typically goes away. A personal policy stays with you and is meant to fill the gaps, not replace what you have.

Isn't my homeowners insurance or PMI enough?

Homeowners insurance protects the house itself from damage. PMI protects the lender, not your family. Neither one makes your mortgage payment if something happens to you.

Do I need a medical exam?

Many carriers now use simplified underwriting. Instead of a nurse visit, they verify your health history through medical databases. You'll answer health questions with me so I can match you with the right company.

Why would I get whole life insurance for my child?

Coverage is at its lowest cost when you're young and healthy. A whole life policy started for a child locks in their rate, guarantees they're insured regardless of future health changes, and builds cash value over time.

How much will it cost?

It depends on your age, health, and how much coverage you need. My rule of thumb is to keep it comfortably under 20% of what's left at the end of the month after bills. We'll find something that fits.
